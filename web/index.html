How to play

  • A green mole lights up on a random key. Press it before it goes dark.
  • Pressing any key that isn't a mole ends the game, and so does a mole that gets away.
  • Each level has 30 rounds. It starts slow and speeds up until there's no pause between moles.
  • After each level the keys flash green, once per mole of the next level. Level 2 has two moles at once, level 3 three, up to six.
  • Every mole you hit is a point. Presses between rounds, and a second press on a mole you already hit, don't count.
